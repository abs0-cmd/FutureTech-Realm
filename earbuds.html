Zero Wireless Earbuds Review: Premium Sound & ANC

1. Overview & First Impressions

The Zero Wireless Earbuds have stepped into the budget-to-midrange audio market with a strong promise: high-definition sound quality, reliable build, and effective noise cancellation without a high price tag.

2. Design, Ergonomics & Comfort

Featuring an ultra-lightweight stem design and matte-finish charging case, Zero Earbuds fit comfortably in ear canals for extended listening sessions. They come with multiple silicone tip sizes to ensure a tight seal and decent passive noise isolation.

3. Sound Performance & Bass Response

Equipped with 10mm dynamic drivers, Zero Earbuds deliver punchy bass and clear vocals. While the sound signature skews slightly warm for daily pop and gaming audio, the overall clarity holds up remarkably well at high volume levels.

4. Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) & Transparency Mode

The ANC performance efficiently cuts down low-frequency background hums like air conditioning or fan noise. Switching to Transparency Mode lets you hear ambient surroundings clearly without removing the earbuds.

5. Battery Life & Fast Charging Support

Zero Earbuds offer up to 6 hours of continuous playback on a single charge with ANC turned off, while the compact charging case extends total battery life to around 28 hours. USB-C fast charging provides about 1 hour of playtime from a 10-minute charge.

6. Microphones, Call Quality & Gaming Latency

Dual beamforming microphones with Environmental Noise Cancellation (ENC) filter out wind noise during voice calls. Low-latency gaming mode drops audio delay sufficiently for smooth mobile gaming sessions.

7. Final Verdict: Worth Buying?

If you are looking for stylish, comfortable wireless earbuds with solid bass performance, decent call quality, and functional ANC, Zero Earbuds stand out as a highly competitive option in their category.
